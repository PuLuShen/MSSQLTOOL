MSSQL Tool - bundled editor background images
==============================================

These six pictures are used as optional SQL editor backgrounds.  They come from
the Reasonix theme packs, which are distributed under the MIT licence, so they may
be bundled and redistributed with this extension.  Attribution is not required by
the licence but is recorded here, and in the About/Settings UI, out of respect for
the authors.

Source : https://github.com/esengine/DeepSeek-Reasonix
Path   : internal/ext/theme/builtin/*.background.webp
Licence: MIT - Copyright (c) 2026 Reasonix Contributors
         https://github.com/esengine/DeepSeek-Reasonix/blob/main/LICENSE

1) sage-breeze.jpg      - Reasonix theme "Sage Breeze"
2) crimson-horizon.jpg  - Reasonix theme "Crimson Horizon"
3) noir-gold.jpg        - Reasonix theme "Noir Gold"
4) violet-starlight.jpg - Reasonix theme "Violet Starlight"
5) spark-notebook.jpg   - Reasonix theme "Spark Notebook"
6) rose-dawn.jpg        - Reasonix theme "Rose Dawn"

Processing: taken from the upstream WebP packs on 2026-11-07 and re-encoded as
progressive JPEG (quality 88) at 2560 px width, because the WPF image decoder
bundled with SSMS cannot read WebP.  No other modification (no cropping, no
retouching) was made.

Notes for the settings UI: the four pictures shipped up to 5.447
(mist-forest, night-sky, mountain-ridge, desert-dunes) were replaced.  A saved
setting that still names one of them is mapped to the closest new picture, so an
upgrade never leaves the editor without a background.

Images supplied by the user are never uploaded or redistributed by the extension;
they stay on the local machine, referenced by path only.
